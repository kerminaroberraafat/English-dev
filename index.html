🗓 The Roadmap

Four-Week Plan

Each week builds on the last — from the sounds you make to the presence you project.

Week 1

Accent & Pronunciation Refinement

  • Focus on sentence stress and word stress
  • Practice connected speech and natural rhythm
  • Repeat ELSA exercises until achieving higher accuracy scores
  • Apply shadowing technique using short audio clips
Week 2

Natural Expressions & Advanced Vocabulary

  • Replace basic expressions with more natural alternatives
  • Practice idiomatic expressions and phrasal verbs
  • Use ChatGPT to generate multiple ways to say the same sentence
  • Focus on sounding less translated and more spontaneous
Week 3

Speaking Under Pressure

  • Practice responding to unexpected topics
  • Deliver 2–3 minute spontaneous speeches
  • Improve clarity while speaking continuously
  • Use ChatGPT for fluency and naturalness evaluation
Week 4

Professional & Confident Communication

  • Deliver structured mini-presentations
  • Practice explaining complex ideas clearly
  • Refine tone and confidence in formal speaking situations
  • Record and compare speaking samples from start to end of month
💡 The Foundation

Key Principles

Hold these close throughout the month — they're your compass, not just rules.

01 🌱

Embrace Mistakes

Mistakes are part of the process — improvement comes from correction, not avoidance.

02 🔥

Consistency Wins

Consistency is more important than perfection. Show up every day, even for ten minutes.

03 🎯

Clarity First

Focus on clarity, confidence, and natural delivery — not impressive vocabulary alone.

04 🤝

AI as Coach

Use AI as a coach, not a replacement for practice. The speaking must always be yours.
